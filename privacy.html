palantir-mail privacy policy

Last updated 2026-11-05.

palantir-mail is a personal email client used only by its owner, with the owner's own Google accounts.

What it accesses

With the account holder's consent, it uses the Gmail API to read messages and labels, change labels, send mail, and manage mail settings such as filters.

Where the data goes

Mail is stored on the owner's own computer. Google sign-in tokens are kept in that computer's system keychain. The app has no server of its own.

What it does not do

Gmail data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI models by this app.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app's local data removes the stored mail.
